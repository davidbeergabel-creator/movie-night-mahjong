Privacy Policy

Movie Night Mahjong

Last updated October 26, 2026

In short: Movie Night Mahjong does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own device.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device only: cleared levels, stars, album cards, calendar stamps, a game in progress and your settings. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else.

You can erase it at any time with Settings › Start everything over, by deleting the app, or by clearing the website's data in your browser.

Sending a board to a friend

If you choose Send this board to a friend, the game makes a link that holds the board, a number that recreates the same deal, which way round it was shown, and how many hints and shuffles you used. It holds nothing about you. The link goes only where you send it, through the app you pick.

Playtest mode

Playtest mode is only turned on by opening a special link during a play test. While it is on, the game keeps a log of game events on the device, such as which levels were played and when a hint was used. The log contains no name, contact details or location, and it stays on the device unless the person running the test chooses to send it.

No outside services

Everything the game needs, including its typefaces, comes with the game itself. It makes no requests to other companies' servers while you play.

Children

The game is made for adults, and it collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the contact details on the game's app store listing.
